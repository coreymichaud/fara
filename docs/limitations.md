# Known limitations

- Public indicators use 2020 Census, ACS 2020–2024, and 2025 retail access. They are not current resident-level observations. The supplied retailer snapshot has no recorded capture date.
- ACS margins of error are visible but are not propagated into statistical intervals. Correlated poverty/access signals do not establish causation. Weights are operator choices.
- Census cartographic boundaries are generalized, not suitable for cadastral or parcel decisions. Interior representative points are not population centers or approved venues. Whole-tract household assignment is coarse and can overstate/understate spatial proximity, especially in large rural tracts.
- A common 1-mile retail threshold supports comparison but has different practical meaning across urban and rural areas. SNAP retailers include convenience stores and do not establish nutrition quality, food affordability, availability, or pantry coverage.
- The existing pantry service network is unknown. No baseline uptake, utilization, incremental improvement, transport accessibility, travel times, route optimization, predicted attendance, or causal reduction in food insecurity is modeled.
- Capacity is household slots for one event. It cannot be interpreted as people, expected demand, food delivered, or households helped. Costs come only from the operator.
- Anonymous check-ins are voluntary convenience samples. A browser cookie, a per-plan per-instance minute limit, a 5,000-response cap, and small-cell suppression provide basic safeguards; they do not guarantee unique people or prevent determined spam/distributed abuse. Closing a survey is transactional with writes.
- Creator tokens are bearer secrets. The URL fragment avoids HTTP request/referrer logs, then exchanges for an HttpOnly, SameSite Strict cookie. Keep private links separately. Lost tokens cannot be recovered without creating a new plan. No accounts or audit recovery service is included.
- JSON persistence is explicitly development-only, serializes only within one process, and is unsuitable for multiple workers or deployment. Production refuses JSON or emulator modes. Firestore IAM and Cloud Run HTTPS are deployment prerequisites.
- The application stores no raw IPs or personal survey fields. Hosting-provider request logs may still contain IPs/metadata; review retention and access before community use. Never enter personal/sensitive information in public operator notes.
- Avenir is used locally when available. The PDF specifies its family/sizes but does not provide a licensed stand-alone webfont bundle. Devices without Avenir use declared system fallbacks; supply licensed WOFF2 assets for identical cross-platform typography.
- No live Cloud Run deployment is claimed without an actual authenticated project, deployment, and smoke test. See the current [verification report](VERIFICATION.md) for measured coverage and browser/container results; thresholds remain 100% even when failing.
