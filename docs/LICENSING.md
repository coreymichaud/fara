# Data and asset publication basis

Reviewed against the supplied files and primary publishers on 7 November 2026. This is the recorded basis for this public-data prototype, not a claim of ownership over Beacon branding or third-party source products.

- The supplied SRAM Read Me identifies USDA Economic Research Service and recommends citation as “U.S. Department of Agriculture, Economic Research Service. Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map.” The application republishes selected **published statistical tract aggregates**, not underlying LandScan population rasters, road datasets, or private organizational inputs. USDA makes these tables available on its [download page](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-access-research-atlas/download-the-data); its [technical methods](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-access-research-atlas/documentation/snap-authorized-retailer-access-map-data-sources-and-technical-methods) identify the mixed source vintages. No restriction on republishing these tabular aggregate facts was found in the supplied Read Me/lookup files. The public-federal-statistics basis is an explicit publication assumption, not a separate Beacon data license.
- ACS statistical facts and official Census cartographic boundaries are U.S. federal public datasets. Only minimized selected statistics and simplified official polygons are bundled. Geometry source: [Census GENZ2020](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2020/shp/cb_2020_12_tract_500k.zip). Attribution identifies Census, year, scale, and transformation.
- Public SNAP retailer points come from the user-supplied snapshot of the [USDA retailer locator](https://www.fna.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator). Names, types, and public coordinates are used; address/contact columns are omitted. No additional publication prohibition was present in the local snapshot. Capture date is unknown.
- Basemap tiles/styles are external [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) services; the rendered map preserves OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap attribution. [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) has its own attribution/license requirements. These tiles are not copied into the container; provider availability is not guaranteed.
- MapLibre’s BSD license is copied with the same-origin worker assets. All other dependency licenses remain with their packages. No AI-generated visual assets are used. Screenshots are actual application captures.
- The supplied Beacon brand guide is a reference asset, not a license to impersonate an official DataKind product. No Beacon logo is fabricated. Font specifications do not grant webfont redistribution rights, and embedded PDF fonts are not extracted.

Raw data and generated web artifacts are excluded from Git. Docker includes only the five minimized web assets under an explicit allowlist. If the challenge organizers supply additional terms restricting publication, **stop deployment**, recheck this basis, and regenerate/publish only assets those terms permit. Bundling data into an image or returning it through an API does not avoid licensing restrictions.
