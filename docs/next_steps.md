# Operational priorities

## Deployment and service ownership

Complete the Google Cloud prerequisites in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md), then build, test, and deploy the local image. Verify Firestore persistence across a redeploy before relying on saved plans. Assign an owner for source refreshes, service monitoring, incident response, and cloud costs. Keep private creator links out of public documents and channels.

## Before planning an event

Verify venue permission, current services, physical accessibility, food storage and handling, staffing, safety, permits, and operating costs with the responsible organization. Ask community partners whether the proposed geography and time are useful. Update stale indicators and record the retailer snapshot capture date. A candidate tract point is only a starting zone.

## Data gaps with the highest value

Validated pantry locations, actual event capacities and service schedules, and comparable operational definitions would support cautious baseline-overlap analysis. Finer population allocation could improve proximity estimates if defensible and licensed. Community sampling and recruitment design matter more than a larger convenience-sample n. Transport modeling would require real Central Florida service and network data and a separately validated model; it is outside the present scope.

## Privacy, accessibility, and maintenance

Review anonymous survey abuse protection, retention and deletion policy, provider log settings, creator-token recovery tradeoffs, accessibility with screen readers, and field usability. Supply licensed Avenir WOFF2 assets for consistent typography across devices. Keep private organizational data and generative AI outside the application’s scope unless a new specification explicitly authorizes them.
